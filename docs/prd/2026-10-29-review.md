# Papers Navigator: UX and product review against the PRD (2026-10-29)

Reviewer: independent agent (Claude). Method: read the PRD and the code (`src/index.md`, `src/my-papers.md`, `src/components/*`, `src/lib/*`, `src/widgets/*`, `src/style.css`), then drove the running preview (http://127.0.0.1:3311) in Chrome at 1600x1000. **Caveat on the phone tests:** `resize_window` did not change the viewport (innerWidth stayed 2133), so I emulated a phone by loading the app in a 400x860 iframe. Media queries and layout respond correctly that way, but real touch behaviour (map brush versus page scroll, tap targets) was **not** verified. The owner's followed list (6 items) was saved first and restored at the end. I made no repo edits.

Verdict up front: **My Papers is good. The Explore "build my list" flow is where the confusion comes from.** The cause is not a single bad control. Explore has **four overlapping definitions of "the current set"** (map brush, score-range histogram, filters, clicked point), plus a fifth threshold ("min score %" in Where to go?). Each one gets its own Follow/Unfollow pair, and the counts on those buttons often do not match the list on screen. On top of that, the followed state is shown almost nowhere on Explore.

**Duplicates and overlaps:**

1. **Five ways to define the current set**: brush, histogram range, filters, clicked point, and Where-to-go min score.
2. **Three bulk Follow/Unfollow pairs**, each over a different set. Only one of them matches the visible list.
3. **Two thresholds** for the same idea (histogram 0.30 versus min score 30%).
4. **Two schedules** (Where to go? in Explore, Itinerary in My Papers) with different shapes and rules.
5. **Three remove controls** for one paper (Explore ★ Following, itinerary ✕, list ★ Unfollow) with three different labels.
6. "Selection" means candidates in the UI ("Selected papers", "Map selection", "Summary of the current selection"), while John calls his list "my selection". That vocabulary clash may be the very complaint.

## 3. Task walkthroughs

### Task 1: Topic → narrow → add → confirm (Planner, laptop)

Steps:

1. Page loads with "Dimensionality reduction" already searched. (Hesitation: did I search this?)
2. Click the "Uncertainty" chip. The map recolours, and the right column says "Selected papers · 5 papers" while the map title says "283 papers by similarity to 'Uncertainty'". Why 5? The reason (the 0.30 cut in the histogram) is below the fold.
3. Brush the teal cluster on the map. The bulk row now reads "Map selection ➕ Follow 39" and "Search results in score range ➕ Follow 5". **The list still shows 5.** Points outside the brush look exactly the same as before.
4. Pick "Search results in score range ➕ Follow 5", the only one that matches what I see. The list buttons flip to "★ Following". The bulk button still reads "➕ Follow 5". The count change ("11 followed") is in the lede, which is scrolled out of view.
5. Confirm what was added: Explore has no way to do it. I have to go to My Papers.

Friction:

- **Blocker for US4.3:** "Map selection Follow 39" would silently add 34 papers that aren't on screen.
- **Major:** You can't tell which bulk button matches the list, and their labels are internal jargon.
- **Major:** No post-action feedback (toast or "Added 5 · Undo"), and no stateful bulk button ("All 5 in your list").
- **Major:** The followed state is invisible on the map.
- **Minor:** The default query returns on reload, and the histogram is below the fold.

### Task 2: All papers by an author, then remove one

Steps:

1. Find the author filter. It is hidden behind "⚙ Options" in the navbar (hesitation: "Options" sounds like settings).

- **Major:** Filters are hidden in a drawer named "Options", with no active-filter chips on the page (US3.3).
- **Minor:** The reason text is noisy. "Unfollow 6" would also remove an author paper you had followed earlier for a topic.

## 4. Top 5 problems

1. **Bulk-follow counts don't match what's listed** ("Follow 39" with 5 shown; "Follow 6" with 3 shown). You can add papers you never saw, and "Unfollow N" isn't an undo. Hurts US4.2, **US4.3**, US2.2, US1.2.
2. **Too many overlapping "current set" definitions** (brush, histogram range, filters, clicked point, min-score %), each with its own controls, and "Selected papers" names the candidates. This is the root of John's complaint. Hurts US4.2, US4.3, US1.2, US2.2, and the PRD §4 vocabulary rule.
3. **The followed state is nearly invisible on Explore:** nothing on the map, no persistent count, bulk buttons aren't stateful, no feedback after adding. Hurts **US4.4**, US4.1, US4.3.
4. **The always-on default query and score cut silently hide results for non-topic tasks** (author or institution lookup shows 3 of 6). Hurts US3.1, US4.2, US1.3.
5. **List-destroying actions without confirmation or undo:** share-banner Replace, import Replace, the itinerary ✕, and bulk Unfollow that also removes earlier follows. Hurts **US7.2**, US4.3, US4.5 (in spirit).

Honourable mentions: Explore on a phone is a long scroll with nested scroll boxes (Q2). No offline support (US6.3). Filters are hidden under "Options" with no active chips (US3.3).

## 5. Improvement proposals

### A. Conservative option: keep the features, fix the semantics (about 1–2 days)

1. **One bulk action, bound to the visible list.** Replace the three pairs with a single button directly above the list: "➕ Add these N to My Papers", where N is exactly the list. Brush, range and filters already compose into the list, so one button covers every case. Stories: US4.2, US4.3. Tradeoff: you lose "add all brushed, ignoring range". If that matters, the list should show it too.
2. **Make it stateful and reversible.** Label it "Add 4 new (2 already in My Papers)", or disable it as "✓ All 6 in My Papers". After the click, show a toast: "Added 4 · Undo". Undo removes only the ids that were _newly_ added in that action, not the whole set. Stories: US4.3, US4.4.
3. **Rename for the PRD vocabulary.** "Selected papers" → "Matching papers (N)". "Map selection" goes away. "Following" → "In My Papers". Navbar → "My Papers (16)" as a badge visible everywhere. Stories: §4, US4.4.
4. **Show followed papers on the map** with a ring or star overlay, always on, independent of Color by. Stories: US4.4, US2.1.
5. **Brush feedback:** dim unbrushed points. Stories: US2.2.
6. **Merge the thresholds:** drive Where to go? from the same candidates as the list (drop the min-score input). Stories: US1.2, US5.3.
7. **Safety:** confirm Replace in an inline two-step ("Replace 14 papers with these 2? [Yes, replace] [Cancel]", no native `confirm`), and offer "Undo" for 10 s after any removal or replace. Keep a one-level backup in localStorage. Stories: US7.2, US4.5.
8. **Active filter chips** above the list, each with ✕, plus "Clear all". Rename "Options" to "Filters". Stories: US3.3.

### B. Bold simplification: what could be removed

- **Remove the map brush as a list filter.** The map becomes a highlight and navigation view: hover, click → scroll to that paper in the list, followed rings. Narrowing happens only through query + relevance cut + filters. That removes "Map selection", the clicked-point mode and the brush/list mismatch. Tradeoff: it weakens US2.2 as written. Could ship a "lasso → add as filter chip 'Map region'" later, so the region becomes just another visible filter.
- **Replace the histogram with a single relevance control**: "Show: Strong matches (12) · Good (31) · All (283)". It's a three-way segmented control, with thresholds computed from the score distribution and shown as counts. Tradeoff: less precise than a brush, but the counts answer US1.2 directly. Keep the histogram as an "advanced" view.
- **Drop Live search toggle, Max papers to list, and navio** from the default UI (move navio to a Stage 3 "Advanced" page). Tradeoff: John's DR/analysis audience loses quick access.
- **Drop Where to go? from Explore** and add a "Preview in My Papers" mode instead: "Try adding these 12" renders the real itinerary with the candidates as ghost entries and conflicts marked, then [Add all] / [Cancel]. That gives one schedule concept, and it doubles as the US4.3 preview. Tradeoff: more work, and Explore no longer answers "where to go" at a glance.
- **Remove the "All followed papers" long list from My Papers** or collapse it by default. The itinerary already lists every paper, and an unscheduled group covers the rest. Keep search there. Tradeoff: sort by reason/added-on moves behind a toggle.
- **Do not search on load.** Start with an empty query showing the program, with the example chips as the call to action. Topic search then can't silently interfere with author lookups. Tradeoff: the first view is less "wow".

### C. Other major items

- **Phone Explore** (Q2): put the search box and list first and collapse the map into "Show map", or use tabs (List | Map). Remove the inner scroll boxes on phones (clamp text with a "more" toggle). Keep a sticky "My Papers (16)" badge.
- **Now/Next polish** (US6.1–6.2): use "now: …" versus "next: …" for the talk. Handle "nothing today" and "conference over" explicitly. Raise the contrast of the conflict text on the blue card.
- **Offline** (US6.3): Framework has no built-in service worker. A small hand-written SW caching `/my-papers`, `papers.json` and the JS would meet the "after first load" requirement. The embeddings and model aren't needed for My Papers.
- **Phone sharing**: on small screens, show "Share list" using `navigator.share` when available, and keep the QR for laptops.

## 6. Suggested answers to the PRD open questions

1. **Schedule preview in Explore?** Yes, but as the _same_ itinerary component in preview mode ("if you add these N"), triggered from the bulk-add button, not as a separate Where to go? widget with its own threshold. Reasoning: the preview is valuable (US5.3, and it doubles as the US4.3 confirmation), but a second schedule shape and threshold is a main source of confusion. If time is short before Nov 3, remove Where to go? rather than keep both.
2. **Bulk add: core or expert?** Core, but **exactly one** bulk action, acting on exactly the visible list, with an undo. Reasoning: "follow everything on uncertainty" and "follow everything by my lab" are John's own stated use cases [J], and they're how a planner builds a 30-paper list in 10 minutes. The problem isn't bulk add. It is three variants over three different sets.
3. **Browser persona first-class?** Secondary. Explore should work fully without a list, but don't add UI just for browsers in Stage 1. Reasoning: the product sentence and every [J] story centre on attendees. The browser story is [C]. Its needs (map, search, filters) are already served by the planner's tools.
4. **Phone Explore: full or search + follow?** Search + list + follow + filters (as chips) by default, with the map behind a "Map" toggle. No brush or histogram on a phone. Reasoning: on the floor, people look up a topic or a name and tap Follow. Brushing a small canvas conflicts with scrolling (I could not verify touch, which is itself a risk), and the current phone layout puts results two screens down.
